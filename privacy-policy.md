# Solar Dominion – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Solar Dominion is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

None. Solar Dominion does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- The app makes no network connections.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services and no third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your campaign progress (Stardust, Legacy upgrades, statistics, audio settings) and the
battle in progress in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device,
except as part of the device backups (iCloud or computer) that you control. Deleting the app deletes
this data.

## Children

The game does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
