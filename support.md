# Solar Dominion – Support

Solar Dominion is a single-player real-time strategy game for iPhone and iPad. It works fully
offline and needs no account.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas: **sbed@protonmail.com**

When reporting a bug, please include your device model, the iOS version and what happened just
before the problem.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my progress saved?**
Yes. Campaign progress and the battle in progress are saved automatically on your device.

**Does the game collect any data?**
No. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.html).

**How do I send support to one of my own planets?**
Select one or more of your planets, then tap another of your planets. Ships fly there and reinforce it.

**Where can I learn the controls?**
Open *How to Play* from the main menu or from the pause menu during a battle.
